World News Words
Learn فارسی Farsi from the news

Tap any word to see and hear it in Farsi, then add it to Words to study it as a flashcard. Solid underlines mark the words you're learning this week (set them on the Today tab); dotted ones are each story's key words.

Each story is a short summary written for language practice, with a link to the original report. New stories arrive every week, and each story's level comes from how common its words are in the frequency dictionary.

Your saved words stay in this browser on this device, and nobody else can see them. To use them on other devices, sign in with Google on the Words tab to keep them in your own Google Drive, or use Save backup. Privacy

Farsi audio is made with an open-source Persian computer voice, so the odd word may sound a little off.
