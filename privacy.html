Privacy

Updated 9 October 2026

World News Words is a free website for learning words from the news. It has no server, no accounts of its own, no ads and no tracking. The people who run it cannot see your words.

What is stored, and where

  • In your browser. The words you save, your study progress and your settings are kept in your browser's local storage on your device. Nobody else can see them.
  • In your own Google Drive, if you sign in with Google. The site creates one file in your Drive named “World News Words – my words.json” and keeps a copy of your words and study progress in it, so you can get them back on another device. The file belongs to you.

How the site uses Google data

  • Signing in asks Google for permission to use only the files this site creates in your Drive (the drive.file permission) and your basic profile.
  • The site cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Your name, email address and picture are shown only to tell you which account is signed in. They are not stored anywhere else or shared.
  • Google's sign-in token stays in your browser's memory, is sent only to Google, and expires after about an hour.
  • Google user data is never sold, shared with anyone, used for ads, or used to train any model.

The site's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Delete the file “World News Words – my words.json” from your Google Drive.
  • Remove the site's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or use Sign out on the Words tab.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove the copy on your device.

Other services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts come from Google Fonts. Those services may keep standard request logs. Each news story links to its original source on another website.
